# Surface brief — Site vitrine GEK INNOVATECH (accueil et pages sœurs)

Scope: whole marketing site (Home, About, Services, Plans, Portfolio, Contact, legal and status pages). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: small business owners in Africa (Bénin first) and the European diaspora, often on a phone on mobile data, deciding whether to trust GEK with their first or next website.
Action: write on WhatsApp (primary); the quote form is the strong second path and appears on the home page itself, not only on /contact.
Proof on hand: published fixed prices and delivery times, one live demo (gek-beaute-elegance), six example concepts, direct contacts. Real figures and testimonials exist but are not supplied yet: their sections render only when `src/data/stats.js` / `src/data/testimonials.js` hold entries.
Constraints: bilingual FR/EN through i18next, light page weight (no background video), brand palette navy, red and white on a light base: navy #1D467B for the brand and text (black only on specific text that truly needs it), on a pale ground #F4F6FA with white cards, red #D42A1C for the WhatsApp buttons and #CC2219 for marks (owner's choice, 2026-11-04, after a light template they liked). Speed on a phone comes first (owner's request, 2026-11-03). Beside the hero headline, a real delivered site on desktop and phone instead of a decorative animation (owner's request, 2026-11-03).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A web agency that proves it builds fast, modern sites by being one: a clear light sheet in blue text, each page opened by a navy band, a real delivered site on screen, published prices, and a single red path to WhatsApp. Refuses the category default of a stock-photo hero, a carousel of claims and invented stats.

OWN-WORLD: Pale ground (#F4F6FA) with white cards, 1px blue-12% hairlines and 24-28px corners, each page opened by a navy gradient band; quiet pill controls with a small leading dot; Barlow (self-hosted) for every word, display at weight 600 with tight tracking, body at 400; navy for the brand, text, links, icons, focus and confirmations; red for the one lit control, checks, live dots and the hero headline's second line. Raise from japanese-high-density-web: pricing and services carry their full real feature lists in dense, tabular, hairline-ruled rows instead of inflated whitespace. Raise from magnetism-electricity-plasma-hand-bent-neon-circuit: only the WhatsApp action is lit (filled red); every other control stays a quiet pill, so red means "this is the path". Raise from ikeda-datamatics: the point terrain is a computed point grid on canvas, frame-stepped on requestAnimationFrame with a fine pointer, drawn once on touch, and fully still under reduced motion. Raise from luxury-fashion-flagship: the hero headline is set large and alone, with no badge, kicker or eyebrow competing.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that GEK builds professional sites, fast, at a fixed price from €89, for businesses like theirs in Africa and the diaspora. They believe it through the published plans, delivery times, a live demo and a direct human contact. They write on WhatsApp, or fill the short quote form on the same page.
